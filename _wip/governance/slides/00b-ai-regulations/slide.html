AI Governance

AI regulations are costly and imposing.

Regulations are multiplying, and most governance tools only watch from outside.

7% of turnover
Prohibited AI practices, such as social scoring, carry fines up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover.
10-year retention
High-risk AI providers must keep technical documentation for ten years (Art. 18).
Outside looking in
Governance tools observe and report — they can't enforce at agent boundaries.
